# Contributors and Joint Ownership

## Hannah Abedin and Erfan Razmand

This project was developed jointly. Both authors share ownership of the
complete engineering workflow, including:

- problem formulation and experiment design;
- digital-twin assumptions and simulator review;
- traffic generation, feature engineering, and leakage prevention;
- forecasting baselines and learned-model comparisons;
- elevator-control baselines and forecast-aware positioning;
- robustness, uncertainty, and failure-mode analysis;
- final-holdout interpretation;
- technical writing, presentation, and reproducibility review.

The repository intentionally avoids artificial contribution percentages or
exclusive ownership claims for individual subsystems. Questions about the
project may be directed to either author through their public professional
profiles.
